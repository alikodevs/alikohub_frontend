Week 1 Sprint Plan & Deliverables

5-Day frontend implementation plan covering foundational student orientation, discussion rubrics, seat-time tracking, and gradebook workflows.

DAY 1 Monday, Nov 2, 2026

Discussion Rubrics & Student Orientation

Milestones & Features
  • Mandatory orientation checklist and course access lock until orientation is completed.
  • Discussion topics with attached grading rubrics and rubric creation.
  • Instructor grading matrix with direct rubric criteria evaluation.
DAY 2 Tuesday, Nov 3, 2026

Active Seat-Time Tracking & Theory Hours

Milestones & Features
  • Active student seat-time tracking with automatic pause during inactivity or tab switching.
  • Student progress indicator showing completed theory hours against required hours.
  • Instructor review view for verified student participation and seat-time records.
DAY 3 Wednesday, Nov 4, 2026

Admissions Clearance & First-Week Attendance

Milestones & Features
  • Student entry requirements checklist for prerequisite documents and background clearances.
  • Instructor clearance dashboard to approve incoming cohort students.
  • First-week attendance verification to confirm active enrollment census.
DAY 4 Thursday, Nov 5, 2026

Gradebook, 80% Passing Standard & Remediation

Milestones & Features
  • Centralized gradebook tracking module scores, quizzes, and discussions per student.
  • Clear indicators for the 80% passing standard across all required units.
  • Remediation assignment workflow for students scoring below the passing standard.
DAY 5 Friday, Nov 6, 2026

Instructor Feedback System & Week 1 Review

Milestones & Features
  • Individualized instructor feedback drawer for student discussion submissions.
  • Student feedback history and evaluation archive with instructor timestamps.
  • End-to-end review and verification of all Week 1 student and instructor workflows.
