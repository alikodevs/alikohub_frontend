WAC 246-841A-440 Compliance

step-by-step showing each requirement and how our LMS frontend delivers it end to end.

POINT 01 General Requirement

Hybrid Format: Theory + Skills Lab + Clinical Correlation

compliance states: Online delivery is only for classroom theory and must connect closely with in-person skills lab and clinical training.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Instructor creates online theory units, videos, PDFs, quizzes, and discussions under My Courses → Curriculum.
  • Instructor logs in-person hands-on competencies in Skills Lab and facility shifts in Clinical Training.
  • Students see all three components linked side by side in Curriculum Crosswalk and My Grades.
POINT 02 Commission Review Access

Dual Access to Instructor & Student Views

compliance states: The commission must see both instructor and student views, including lessons, quizzes, discussions, feedback tools, and gradebook.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Commission Auditors log into a read-only staff view showing Gradebook, Submissions, Rubrics, Program Evaluations, and Compliance Audit.
  • Auditor clicks Preview Student View in the header (or Inspect Course Player (Read-Only) on a course).
  • Auditor browses lessons, readings, quizzes, and forums in read-only mode, then clicks Back to Audit Portal.
POINT 03 Class Size Rule

1:30 Student-to-Instructor Ratio Cap

compliance states: Asynchronous online cohorts cannot have more than 30 students per instructor.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Instructor opens My Courses → Cohorts and clicks Create Cohort.
  • Cohort setup caps Max Students between 1 and 30 and assigns the supervising instructor.
  • Ratio compliance is visible to reviewers on the Compliance Audit page.
POINT 04 Completion Timeline

3-Month (90-Day) Program Limit

compliance states: Students must finish the entire program within 3 months, except approved 10-month high school programs.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Create / Edit Cohort blocks date ranges over 90 days unless the 10-Month High School Calendar Program checkbox is enabled.
  • Students see remaining cohort days and pacing status (On Track / At Risk) in My Schedule and My Grades.
  • Instructors monitor every student's pacing across cohorts inside Gradebook.
POINT 05 Institutional Quality

Online Training Capability & Quality Metrics

compliance states: Applicants must show institutional support, online quality standards, and program review outcomes.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Active student seat-time is automatically logged while studying lessons and exported in Compliance Audit.
  • Students rate course quality, LMS platform, and instruction in Program Evaluation.
  • Instructors, Admins, and Auditors review survey averages and program compliance dossiers together in one place.
POINT 06 Syllabus Alignment

Curriculum & Textbook Reading Correlation

compliance states: Online lessons must align directly with assigned textbook readings.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Instructor opens any lesson in My Courses → Curriculum and clicks the Textbooks & Readings tab.
  • Instructor registers the course textbook and links exact chapter names and page ranges to that lesson.
  • Students see required textbook chapters directly inside every lesson player and in Curriculum Crosswalk.
POINT 07 Faculty Supervision

Approved RN Supervision & Active Instructor Support

compliance states: Approved RN instructors must supervise and interact with students across theory, skills lab, and clinical shifts.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Instructor enters their RN license number, expiration date, LTC experience, and documents in Instructor Settings.
  • Admin reviews and approves RN faculty credentials under Admin → Faculty & Credentials.
  • Approved instructors post Announcements, run live Q&As in Schedules, grade discussions, check off lab skills, and verify clinical hours.
POINT 08 Entry Requirements

Student Selection & Admissions Clearance

compliance states: Programs must verify entry requirements and enrollment records before students begin.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Student opens Admissions and uploads all 6 entry documents (Background Check, TB Test, Immunizations, BLS/CPR, Photo ID, Diploma/GED).
  • Student reviews statutory disclosures and signs the electronic Enrollment Agreement on screen.
  • Instructor opens Admissions (Admissions & Document Review), inspects each document, and approves or returns for correction.
POINT 09 Pre-Start Requirement

Mandatory Non-Credit Orientation

compliance states: Students must complete live or in-person orientation before starting, and orientation hours cannot count toward program hours.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Before entering lessons, students open Orientation to confirm policies, schedule expectations, online conduct, and LMS navigation.
  • Course lessons stay locked until orientation is marked complete.
  • Study time tracking only starts inside actual lessons, so orientation time never counts toward course hours.
POINT 10 Week-1 Attendance

First-Week Academic Assignment & Attendance Census

compliance states: Programs must use an academic-based assignment in Week 1 to verify student attendance.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Students complete their Week-1 academic activity inside the first module lessons.
  • Instructor opens Attendance Census to review Verified Present vs. At-Risk / No-Show students.
  • Instructor exports the cohort attendance record cleanly from the census page.
POINT 11 Unit Interaction

Unit Asynchronous Discussions & Grading Rubrics

compliance states: Each unit needs an asynchronous discussion tied to outcomes with student interaction, instructor feedback, and a grading rubric.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Instructor creates or clones structured multi-criteria rubrics under Rubrics.
  • Instructor creates a discussion topic per unit in My Courses → Curriculum and attaches a rubric.
  • Students preview the rubric criteria inside the lesson's Unit Discussion Forum, post replies to classmates, and receive rubric-scored feedback.
POINT 12 Peer Collaboration

Independent Student Study Groups

compliance states: Students must be able to meet independently in peer study groups.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Student opens Study Groups from the sidebar and selects their cohort.
  • Student clicks New Study Group to share a topic and meeting details, or joins an existing group in one click.
  • Students can leave, update, or organize group study sessions independently.
POINT 13 Student Remediation

Individualized Written Instructor Feedback

compliance states: Students needing help must receive robust, personalized instructor feedback rather than only computer-generated scores.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Instructor opens Submissions, opens a student's attempt, and scores each question using text feedback or an interactive rubric matrix.
  • Instructor enters holistic attempt comments and clicks Save Overall Feedback to store them in the gradebook.
  • Students read their personalized feedback and rubric breakdowns under Peer Grading (graded submissions) and My Grades.
POINT 14 Time Management

Unified Schedule of Theory, Skills & Clinical Deadlines

compliance states: Students must receive an organized schedule covering classroom theory, skills lab, clinical dates, and paced deadlines.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Instructor schedules Live Sessions, Recordings, Q&As, Office Hours, and Workshops by cohort in Schedules (with Edit & Delete).
  • Instructor creates facility dates in Clinical Training and posts key updates in Announcements.
  • Student opens My Schedule to see upcoming live classes, clinical facility shifts, and cohort deadline pacing in one view.
POINT 15 Assessment Integrity

Exam Identity Verification & Proctoring Security

compliance states: Programs must verify each student's identity on online exams and maintain security controls during testing.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Before an exam starts in Lessons, student uploads a Government Photo ID, takes a live Webcam snapshot, and signs the integrity pledge.
  • During the exam, the player enforces a countdown timer, blocks copying, and counts tab/window switches.
  • Instructor reviews the Photo ID, Webcam snapshot, and blur flags in Submissions (and Admin in Compliance & Audits) to approve or mark unverified.
POINT 16 Platform Operations

Technical Support, Privacy, Security & Data Backups

compliance states: Programs must provide reliable technical support, LMS training/specs, privacy/security safeguards, and data backups.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Student opens Support Desk to check supported browsers, hardware/network specs, response hours, privacy/security safeguards, and backup policy.
  • Student clicks Submit Support Ticket for technical issues and tracks staff responses.
  • Instructors and Admins triage and resolve open tickets inside Support Desk and Admin → Support.
POINT 17 Graduation & Reporting

Electronic Gradebook, Skills Lab, Clinical Hours & Certificates

compliance states: All Nursing Assistant standards (80% theory, lab checkoffs, clinical hours, gradebook, and records) must work together for program completion.
Implementation(Step-by-Step Flow)
  • Instructor records Core Skills in Skills Lab, logs 40+ clinical hours in Clinical Training, and monitors cohort readiness or exports CSVs in Gradebook.
  • Student tracks Theory (≥80%), Skills Lab, and Clinical milestones in My Grades and submits Program Evaluation.
  • Once all gates pass, student generates a printable certificate in Certifications, verifiable publicly at WABON Credential Verification (/verify-certificate).
