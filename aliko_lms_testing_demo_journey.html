AlikoHub LMS Testing Journey

End-to-end demo script across Instructor, Student, Admin, and Commission Auditor accounts to test every implemented flow live.

JOURNEY 01 Instructor → Student → Instructor → Student

Rubrics, Unit Discussion Post & Rubric Grading

Step-by-Step Click Flow
  • 1 Instructor Open Rubrics from the sidebar → click Create Rubric → define criteria and point levels (or pick a starter template) → save your Discussion Rubric.
  • 2 Instructor Open My Courses → open your course → go to the Activities tab → expand a module → click Unit Discussion & Rubrics.
  • 3 Instructor Click Create Unit Discussion Topic → write the clinical prompt → select your rubric under Attach Standardized Grading Rubric → click Publish Discussion Topic.
  • 4 Student Switch to Student account → open My Learning → open the lesson → click Go to Unit Discussion Forum.
  • 5 Student Click View Rubric Criteria to see expectations → type your message/answer → click Post Contribution (and reply to peers with Reply → Post Peer Reply).
  • 6 Instructor Return to My Courses → Activities → [Module] → Unit Discussion & Rubrics → see the student's post → click Grade with Rubric → pick criterion scores, add written feedback, and save.
  • 7 Student Back in Student Unit Discussion Forum, see the earned points badge, feedback, and View Rubric Evaluation (and updated score in My Grades).
JOURNEY 02 Student → Instructor → Student

Admissions 6-Document Clearance, E-Sign & Orientation Gate

Step-by-Step Click Flow
  • 1 Student Open Admissions from the sidebar → upload all 6 documents (Background Check, TB Test, Immunizations, BLS/CPR, Photo ID, Diploma/GED).
  • 2 Student Review disclosures and sign the electronic Enrollment Agreement on screen.
  • 3 Instructor Open Admissions (Admissions & Document Review) → inspect the student's 6 uploaded files → click Approve (or Reject with remediation reason).
  • 4 Student Try opening a course lesson before orientation → blocked cleanly by the Orientation Gate → go to Orientation, complete the checklist and attendance attestation.
  • 5 Student Enter Lessons smoothly; seat-time tracking now begins only inside real lessons.
JOURNEY 03 Instructor → Student

Course Setup: 1:30 Cohort Cap, 90-Day Window, Textbooks & Schedules

Step-by-Step Click Flow
  • 1 Instructor In My Courses → Cohorts, click Add Cohort → confirm fixed capacity is locked at 30 Students Max and test the 90-day date validation (or check 10-Month High School Calendar Program).
  • 2 Instructor Switch to the Activities tab → select a lesson → open the Textbooks & Readings tab → register the course textbook and link specific chapters & pages.
  • 3 Instructor Open Schedules → create a Live Session, Q&A, or Office Hours slot for the cohort (and test editing/deleting a session).
  • 4 Student Open the lesson to see Required Textbook Readings inline, open Curriculum Crosswalk for the full course map, and open My Schedule to see classes + cohort deadline pacing.
JOURNEY 04 Student → Instructor → Admin → Student

Proctored Exam ID/Webcam Check, Anti-Cheat & Personalized Feedback

Step-by-Step Click Flow
  • 1 Student Open an exam lesson → upload a Government Photo ID, take a live Webcam snapshot, check the integrity pledge → start the exam.
  • 2 Student Answer questions while countdown timer, copy-block, and tab-switch tracking run → submit exam.
  • 3 Instructor Open Submissions → select the student attempt → view Photo ID, Webcam snapshot, and tab-blur incidents → click Approve Proctor Session (or Mark Unverified).
  • 4 Instructor Enter individualized feedback per question (or score via rubric) and click Save Overall Feedback.
  • 5 Admin Optionally open Admin → Compliance & Audits to inspect flagged proctored exam sessions across the institution.
  • 6 Student Open Peer Grading (Instructor Feedback) → click View details to read written comments and rubric results.
JOURNEY 05 Instructor → Admin → Instructor → Student

RN License Credentials, In-Person Skills Lab & Clinical Hours

Step-by-Step Click Flow
  • 1 Instructor Open Settings → enter RN License Number, Expiration Date, Years of LTC Experience, and upload certificates → submit.
  • 2 Admin Open Admin → Faculty & Credentials → approve the instructor's RN credentials.
  • 3 Instructor Open Skills Lab → optionally click Register Core Skill to add skills → pick Cohort & Student → record in-person Pass/Fail checkoffs and notes.
  • 4 Instructor Open Clinical Training → click Schedule Clinical Shift → enter facility, date, and hours → later log student attendance & hours completed.
  • 5 Student Open Skills Lab, Clinical Training, and My Schedule to see passed competencies, verified clinical hours, and upcoming shifts.
JOURNEY 06 Student → Instructor / Admin → Student

Peer Study Groups, Cohort Announcements & Technical Support

Step-by-Step Click Flow
  • 1 Student Open Study Groups → click New Study Group (or join an active peer group) to collaborate independently with classmates.
  • 2 Instructor Open Announcements in the instructor sidebar → publish an update (students view it under Announcements).
  • 3 Student Open Support Desk → inspect browser/hardware specs, Privacy & Data Security, and Reliability & Data Backup policies → submit a ticket.
  • 4 Admin / Staff Triage and resolve the ticket in Support Desk or Admin → Support so the student sees the updated response.
JOURNEY 07 Commission Auditor Inspection Loop

Auditor Read-Only Review Across Staff & Student Views

Step-by-Step Click Flow
  • 1 Auditor Sign in as COMMISSION_AUDITOR → review Gradebook, Attendance Census, Submissions, Rubrics, Program Evaluations, and Compliance Audit (notice edit/create actions stay locked).
  • 2 Auditor Click Preview Student View in the top header to enter the student portal without enrollment or orientation blocks.
  • 3 Auditor Open any course → click Inspect Course Player (Read-Only) → review videos, required readings, quizzes, and discussion threads safely with no accidental submissions.
  • 4 Auditor Click Back to Audit Portal in the student header to return immediately to compliance reports.
JOURNEY 08 Instructor → Student → Public

Consolidated Gradebook, Evaluation Survey, Certificate & Verification

Step-by-Step Click Flow
  • 1 Instructor Open Gradebook → verify theory (≥80%), discussions, 22/22 skills, 40+ clinical hours, and export the cohort CSV.
  • 2 Student Open My Grades to verify all pass thresholds → open Program Evaluation to submit 1–5 ratings on course, platform, and instructor.
  • 3 Instructor Open Program Evaluations to confirm the cohort survey metrics and comments updated live.
  • 4 Student Open Certifications (or click View certificate on a completed course) → click Generate Certificate → print or open the public verification link.
  • 5 Public Anyone can visit /verify-certificate (no login needed), enter the Certificate Number, and verify official WABON completion status live.
