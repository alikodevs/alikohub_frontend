CONTRACT DRIFT

Backend Postman Drift, Cohort Fallbacks & Role Isolation

What Is Built Across the Platform
  • Role Isolation & Live Data Truth: usePortalMode, InstructorRoute, AdminRoute, and InstructorSidebar cleanly isolate Student, Instructor, Admin (/admin/*), and read-only COMMISSION_AUDITOR views without mixing student attendance inside Admin. Recent updates purged hardcoded hour limits, static skill counts, and decorative title badges.
  • Defensive Path & Response Normalization: Services such as admissionsService, orientationService, and discussionService handle both /academy/* and root API prefixes and normalize array vs. wrapped response shapes.
System-Wide Risks & Mismatches
  • Master Postman vs. Live Validation Schemas: Frontend comments explicitly note live controller/Joi DTO shapes that differ from Postman sample bodies in Admissions (6 file URLs + signatureUrl + status vs. Postman flat fields/decision), Orientation (orientationAttended/netiquetteSigned vs. orientationDate/syllabusAcknowledged), Rubrics (name/type/title vs. title/courseId/maxScore), and Clinical (hoursCredited/hoursCompleted/competencyNotes vs. totalHours/attendedHours/clinicalNotes).
  • Commission Auditor Locked Out of Student Lesson Player: InstructorLayout hides Switch to Student when isAuditor is true, OrientationGate omits COMMISSION_AUDITOR from isPrivileged, and LessonView blocks anyone with !isEnrolled — preventing Commission auditors (and un-enrolled staff) from viewing Student lessons, quizzes, and inline discussions.
  • Silent cohortId = 1 Fallbacks: CourseDetails supports self-paced enrollments without a cohort; when cohortId is absent, OrientationGate and the 60s seat-time heartbeat in LessonView fall back to cohortId = 1.
MODULE 01 Admissions, Orientation & Week-1 Census
MOSTLY COMPLETE

Student Selection, Mandatory Non-Credit Orientation & Attendance Census

Implemented Frontend Flows
  • 6-Document Clearance & RCW 1.80 E-Sign: AdmissionsClearancePage collects Background Check, TB Test, Immunizations, BLS/CPR, Photo ID, and HS Diploma/GED (via /upload -> /files/upload -> /content/upload fallback), signs the Enrollment Agreement + 4 statutory disclosures (POST /admissions/sign-agreement), and guides students to Orientation.
  • Instructor Document Verification & Week-1 Census: InstructorAdmissionsPage checks 6/6 prerequisites and approves/rejects with remediation notes; CohortCensusPage tracks VERIFIED_PRESENT, PENDING_VERIFICATION, NO_SHOW_AT_RISK, and ADMINISTRATIVE_DROP alongside WABON Dossier export.
  • Non-Credit Orientation Gate: OrientationChecklistPage covers program policies, netiquette, LMS navigation, and live attendance attestation; OrientationGate gates /learn/:id, while seat-time heartbeats only run inside LessonView (excluding orientation time).
Gaps Found
  • Missing Week-1 Gate Toggle in Exercise Authoring: LessonView and CurriculumCompliancePage consume isWeekOneAttendanceGate, but ExerciseModal has no checkbox to mark a question/exercise as the Week-1 attendance gate.
  • No Inline Administrative Drop Action in Census: CohortCensusPage displays at-risk/no-show counts without an instructor drop button (enrollment cancellation DELETE /enrollments/:id only lives in Admin).
MODULE 02 Rubrics Engine & Asynchronous Discussions
WELL IMPLEMENTED

Standardized Multi-Criteria Rubrics & Unit Discussion Forums

Implemented Frontend Flows
  • Full Rubric Lifecycle: RubricsManagementPage, RubricBuilderModal (with Infection Control & CNA Clinical Case starter templates), RubricSelector, RubricGradingMatrix (POST /rubrics/evaluate with client fallback), RubricCriteriaTable, and RubricStudentResultView support create, preview, type filter (DISCUSSION/ASSIGNMENT), cloning (POST /rubrics/:id/clone), and auditor read-only locks.
  • Embedded Unit Discussions: Instructors create unit discussion topics and bind rubrics inside CurriculumTab (canCreateTopic={true}); students access per-unit forums directly from LessonView, inspect attached rubric criteria upfront, post threaded replies (parentId), and review instructor rubric breakdowns once graded via POST /discussions/posts/:postId/grade.
Gaps Found
  • Hardcoded UI Pass Benchmarks: RubricGradingMatrix and SubmissionDetail check >= 80% while DiscussionForumView flags isSubstantive at >= 70%, rather than reading rubric-specific passingScore values from the backend.
  • No Publish Completeness Check & Module Fallback: Nothing prevents publishing a module with zero discussion topics, and LessonView passes activeMod?.id || 1 if active module lookup is missing.
MODULE 03 In-Person Skills Lab & Clinical Hours
ALIGNED WITH GAPS

22-Skill CNA Competency Matrix & Facility Clinical Shift Tracking

Implemented Frontend Flows

MODULE 04 Curriculum Crosswalk, Textbooks, Cohorts & Schedules
AUTHORING GAPS

Hybrid Crosswalk, Required Readings, 1:30 Ratio & 90-Day Pacing

Implemented Frontend Flows
  • Dynamic Curriculum Crosswalk View: CurriculumCompliancePage reads live /curriculum-crosswalk/matrix/course/:courseId and /curriculum-crosswalk/textbooks/course/:courseId with backend-derived track filters and zero hardcoded hour fallbacks.
  • Cohort & Teaching Schedules: CreateCohortModal and CohortsTab create cohorts (POST /cohorts); ScheduleTab, instructor Schedules, and StudentSchedules display LIVE, RECORDING, Q_AND_A, OFFICE_HOURS, and WORKSHOP sessions.
Gaps Found
  • Dead Textbook & Reading Assignment Hooks: useCreateTextbookMutation (3.6.1) and useAssignReadingMutation (3.6.2) exist in useCurriculumCrosswalk.ts but have zero callers — instructors cannot add textbooks or attach chapter/page readings via UI, and LessonView never calls getLessonReadings to display aligned textbook readings inline.
  • Missing Cohort Pacing & Ratio Controls: Neither CreateCohortModal nor CohortsTab collects maxStudents (cap 30), assigns instructorId, validates the 90-day completion limit, or supports the 10-month High School program calendar exception.
  • Incomplete Schedule Controls & Missing Instructor Announcements: ScheduleTab lacks meetingUrl, cohortId, edit/delete actions, and unified unit/lab/clinical deadline pacing; Announcement management only exists in Admin (AdminAnnouncements), not Instructor routes.

60s Engagement Heartbeats, Identity Attestation & Proctor Audits
